Finora Privacy Policy

Smart Money Management

Finora ("we", "our", or "the app") is an AI-powered personal finance application designed to help users manage their personal financial information, including income, expenses, and transactions.

1. Information We Collect

Google Sign-In

When you choose to sign in with Google, Finora may receive basic profile information from your Google Account, including:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your profile picture

This information is used to create and manage your Finora account and to identify you when you sign in.

Finora does not request access to your Gmail messages, contacts, Google Drive files, or other Google services that are not required for authentication.

Email and Password Login

If you create an account using email and password, Finora uses your email address and password for account authentication.

Financial Information

Finora allows you to enter financial information such as:

  • Transaction titles
  • Income amounts
  • Expense amounts
  • Transaction types

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected by Finora may be used to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Authenticate your identity
  • Provide login functionality
  • Display your account information
  • Record and manage your income and expenses
  • Calculate and display financial information
  • Improve the functionality and user experience of Finora

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Storage of Information

Finora currently uses local storage on your device for application data such as locally stored user and transaction information.

Google authentication is handled through Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication.

Finora does not intentionally store your Google password.

4. Google User Data

When you use Google Sign-In, Finora receives only the basic information necessary for authentication and account identification, such as your name, email address, and profile information.

Google user data is used only for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Finora does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

5. Third-Party Services

Finora uses trusted third-party services to provide authentication functionality, including:

  • Google Sign-In
  • Firebase Authentication

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by Finora. Authentication is handled using Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored application information may remain on your device until it is removed by the user or the application data is cleared.

If you want your account or personal information removed, you may contact us using the contact information provided below.

8. Children's Privacy

Finora is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as Finora's features and services change.

Any updated version will include a revised "Last Updated" date.
